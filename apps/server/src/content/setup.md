Enter the startup secret configured on the native server, then select your disc folder.

The shared disc loader checks your image in this browser. Only the server’s required resources are uploaded to private temporary storage. They are never offered for download and are removed when the server shuts down.

After preparation, choose the map and human player count. To play alone against the original AI, select one opponent; this selects one human and leaves the other seats empty. Use **None** for the usual human-only match.
